# SEO action plan for RRY Malaysia

## Implemented locally

1. Give every public route its own title, description, canonical URL and social preview URL.
2. Build product metadata from the published Sanity title, description and image.
3. Use Sanity product update timestamps in the sitemap; omit guessed static-page update dates.
4. Remove unsupported in-stock and search-action claims from structured data.

## After deployment

1. Verify that Sanity's published company name, address, phone, email and WhatsApp number are accurate. Then update Google Business Profile and other business listings to match.
2. Check Google Search Console's Pages report for indexing and canonical selection. Submit `https://rrymalaysia.com/sitemap.xml` if it is not already submitted.
3. Recheck key pages after Google recrawls them; metadata improvements do not guarantee a particular ranking.
4. Measure mobile Core Web Vitals in Search Console or PageSpeed Insights. The API was rate limited during this audit, so no performance claim is made.
5. Use Search Console queries and customer language to identify any new service or product content worth writing. Avoid speculative location pages or duplicate copy.
